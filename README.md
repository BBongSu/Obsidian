# My Obsidian Knowledge Base

Welcome to my personal knowledge base, managed with [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md/). This vault serves as a central repository for my work, learning, personal life, and interests.

## 📂 Structure Overview

The vault is organized into several key categories to keep information structured and easily accessible.

### 💻 Tech & Work
Notes related to software development, work projects, and technical knowledge.
- **Scrum**: Agile methodologies and scrum notes.
- **Cafe24**: Work related to Cafe24 platform.
- **ChatGPT**: AI prompts, conversations, and learnings.
- **개발 (Development)**: General programming and development notes.
- **컴퓨터 (Computer)**: Hardware, software, and CS concepts.
- **프로세스 (Process)**: Workflows and productivity processes.
- **질문 창고 (Question Bank)**: Collection of technical questions and answers.

### 💰 Finance
Financial planning, investment, and asset management.
- **ETF**: Exchange-Traded Funds tracking and analysis.
- **부동산 (Real Estate)**: Real estate market notes and listings.
- **회계 (Accounting)**: Accounting principles and personal records.

### 📚 Learning
Academic subjects and continuous learning materials.
- **강의 (Lectures)**: Notes from various courses and lectures.
- **과제 (Assignments)**: Project and assignment drafts.
- **국어 (Korean)**: Language studies.
- **사회 (Social Studies)**: Humanities and social sciences.
- **영어 (English)**: English language learning.
- **학습 (Learning)**: General learning strategies and notes.

### 🧘 Life & Personal
Personal thoughts, memories, and life management.
- **일기 (Journal)**: Daily logs and reflections.
- **신혼생활 (Newlywed Life)**: Planning and memories.
- **자서전 (Autobiography)**: Life story drafts.
- **독서 (Reading)**: Book reviews and reading lists.
- **건강 (Health)**: `건강.md` - Health tracking and tips.
- **여행 (Travel)**: `여행.md` - Travel plans and logs.

### 🎨 Hobbies & Content
Interests, hobbies, and content creation.
- **게임 (Games)**: Gaming notes and guides.
- **뉴스 (News)**: Clippings and current events.
- **음악 (Music)**: Music theory, playlists, or lyrics.
- **자동차 (Cars)**: Automotive interests.
- **커피 (Coffee)**: Brewing guides and bean notes.
- **동물 (Animals)**: `동물.md` - Pet or animal related notes.
- **아이디어 (Ideas)**: Creative brainstorming.
- **Youtube**: Content planning for YouTube.
- **블로그 (Blog)**: Blog post drafts and ideas.

### ⚙️ System & Utilities
Obsidian configuration and helper files.
- **Assets**: Images and media attachments.
- **Templates**: Markdown templates for new notes.
- **Tags**: Tag taxonomy and organization.
- **Vault.md**: Vault root note.
